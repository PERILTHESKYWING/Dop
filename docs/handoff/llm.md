# LLM usage
Summary: Gemini Flash (free tier) explains numbers that KataGo and the data supply; code checks its claims.

- Proxy: `api/llm.ts` → `shared/llmServer.ts`, task-based (`ask-position`, `chat`, `forge-problems`, ...). Env: `LLM_API_KEY`, `LLM_MODEL`, `LLM_PROVIDER`.
- Pattern everywhere: gather KataGo/pro/user-stat facts in the browser first, one model call, a code checker (`shared/chat.ts` checkAnswer, `shared/ask.ts`) verifies coordinates and figures, at most one correction round.
- Coach chat `#/chat`: up to 3 extra KataGo probes batched, then a final call; Deep mode adds a review pass only when there are checkable claims.
- Forge problems: positions come only from KataGo-verified `public/problems/bank.json`; the LLM writes text in one batched call per set (`shared/forgeWriter.ts`), with fallback text when no LLM.
- Never tested against real Gemini from the sandbox (no key); tests stub `/api/llm`.
- Kami (`kami/`): task `kami-write` in `shared/kami.ts`, see `kami.md`. Only the ultraGO thinking level calls it (one call per answer). Since 2026-10-20 the prompt asks for Kiai's own engine voice (the draft's sentences and order, only clearer; no chat habits, no clause dashes, emoji only where the draft has one), and `kiaiVoice` in `kami/src/lib/writer.ts` strips assistant openers/closers and dashes before `checkAnswer`. XIAO: never show the provider's name in the app.
- Sharp positions (2026-10-23, swarm critics: "Plenty of room to play here!" over a 1% win chance at move 34): `situationOf` marks `sharp` when one side leads by 5+ points or has a win chance of 10% or less (`lopsided`), the last move touched the mover's stones (`contact`), or KataGo's first choice has 60%+ of the visits after the first few moves. Then `teach.ts` (engine text, the no-LLM path) says who leads by about how much, what the contact play was ("White's Q11 attaches to the black stone at R11"), KataGo's move and the local fight from its own line ("White's main reply in KataGo's line is the cut at R10, and Black continues with the extension at Q9"); no cheer, no "play your style", no closing question. Calm, even openings keep XIAO's relaxed voice, without the "There are N stones" filler. Sente needs KataGo's line to show the reply locally and then the mover elsewhere (`senteInLine`). Shapes: a move straight on from one's own stone into an opponent stone is a block, a step toward the edge on the 1st/2nd line a descent (`shapes.ts`). The writer prompt (PROMPT_VERSION 5, same text in `shared/kami.ts`) gets "sharp: …" in its tone line, and `wordingProblems` in `answerCheck.ts` (part of `checkAnswer`) rejects calm phrases, "!" and a closing question in a sharp position, the wrong side ahead or "close" at 5+ points, "decided" before `gameDecided` (late, from the point lead), "sente" no concept or draft shows, and a shape word that is not the shape the point makes where it is played; a rejected wording shows the engine text. Board labels: a lopsided read shows each candidate's loss against KataGo's choice (0.0, −0.9, −1.3; `kami/src/lib/candLabels.ts`), and "the game is decided" only when `gameDecided` agrees. Tests: `tests/kiai-sharp.test.ts` (real b10 reads of the move-34 position in `tests/fixtures/kiai-move34-reads.json`).
