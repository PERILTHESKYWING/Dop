## Pacing
- Cut rate: slow open (1.4 s, 7 s), longer holds mid-video, then a burst of 4 cuts in 3 s at 1:15-1:18 on the "I love anime" peak. Cut length varies on purpose: the burst only lands because the minute before it is slow.
- Only 6 of 20 cuts fall on a sound hit; most cuts are on sentence or idea boundaries (caption change), not on a beat. No steady beat.
- Motion per shot is mostly "still" or "slow": the picture rests while she talks; "moving" shots are the chef gag and the finale.
- Shots with no character (blank page + caption) are used as breath, about 2-3 s each.

## Camera and framing
- Always flat, front-on, locked off; no perspective, no 3D. Variety comes from the *crop*: extreme close-up (face fills frame, eyes ~1/3 up), waist-up, full body small, tiny chibi in empty space.
- The character sits off-centre (usually right third) and the other third carries a prop, label or text. Empty white is the background; one frame uses a collage.
- Close-ups on the biggest emotion beats (shock, "especially BL", embarrassed). Wide only for "meet the character".
- Emotion changes between shots by switching *drawing*, not by morphing one drawing: new shot = new pose/expression.

## Motion
- Hand-drawn, traced look: line boil on held poses (line shimmers on twos), slight drift of the whole head; hair and bangs move a little on their own.
- Clean: no jitter on cuts, no popping features; every frame is a complete, deliberate drawing.
- Gag beats get one big pose change (shrinking to chibi, cat eyes, sparkles), then hold.

## Character (Pegi, the thing to be beautiful)
- Single-weight dark plum/maroon line (#3d0f1f-ish) on white; no colour fills, except tiny flag/prop colours. Line is thick and confident, visibly varying width, with scribbled shading in the eyes and sketchy hair strands.
- Proportions: young woman, ~6-7 heads tall full body, slim, wide-leg trousers + sleeveless top (full body) or tee/chef jacket.
- Head: round, soft chin, short messy bob with side-swept bangs falling over the forehead and outward flicks at the ends, a single aho-ge antenna strand on top; hair is drawn as many loose strokes, not one filled shape.
- Face: large round eyes with thick upper lash lines and extra lash ticks, small round nose (just a circle), small mouth (open "o", flat smile, or wavy), blush as 3-5 short diagonal hatch marks on each cheek, light brows that carry mood. Heart-shaped glasses as a prop in the "BL" beat.
- Expression set seen: shock, blank, spiral-eyed panic, smug (tongue out), shy (cross-hatch blush + eyes closed), heart eyes, cat eyes, closed-eye smile, sulk/pout.

## Text and captions
- Captions: bold white sans, black stroke, bottom centre, 1-2 lines, word-for-word with the speech, changing at each phrase, always readable (never covered).
- Big title text is drawn by hand (outline letters) as a character reveal, only once.
- Props carry their own small hand-written labels with arrows ("Never animated before", "Embarrassed", checklist).

## Sound (measured only)
207 sound hits over 114 s, loudness steady mid-high (4-6 of 9) with dips at 0:47 and 1:09 (pauses) and the very end. This is a talking voice-over, not music-led. The audio was not heard, so no claim on voice style.

## What made it work (for the rules)
1. One reaction hook in the first 1.4 s, then an extreme close-up of the face by 1.5 s.
2. Every shot has one subject and one prop/label; the rest is empty white.
3. Crops change constantly even when the drawing is simple.
4. Slow long holds then a short burst: contrast keeps attention.
5. The character is attractive on purpose: big eyes, lashes, blush, soft messy hair, one signature accessory per beat.
6. Gags are drawn, not animated: swap to chibi/cat-eyes/chef costume for a held beat.
7. Blank "breath" frames and prop-only frames reset attention.
8. Captions at the same place, same style, for the entire video.
