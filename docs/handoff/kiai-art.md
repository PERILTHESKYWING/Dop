# Kiai art (renders)
Summary: Kiai's flat board skin, the landing's sky-and-table plate and the live 3D board's textures come from `tools/kiai-art/` (Blender 5 as a Python module, Cycles on CPU, plus numpy). All materials and the clouds are procedural; nothing is downloaded. Outputs go to `kami/public/kiai/` (committed, small). The razorbill (Ikki) and its flight intro are gone: dropped 2026-10-18, and their scripts deleted 2026-10-19 when XIAO asked to scrub the mascot completely.

## Setup
- `python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install bpy pillow numpy` (Python 3.11+, about 400 MB). ffmpeg for the video.
- Run each script with that Python from `tools/kiai-art/`. The hero's position is `pos.json` (34 moves of bench game g70dd864b0bcbfc05; the same stones are in `kami/src/ui/landingDemo.ts`).

## Scripts
- `kl.py` shared: kaya (masame grain, amber matched to XIAO's reference board; `contrast`, `palette`, `horizontal` for the sides), slate and clamshell stones (lathed double-convex), sky, sun, camera. `woodtest.py OUT` renders quick swatches and prints mean colour and grain strength (reference board top: about rgb 164/100/44 with lines; our wood 180/116/61).
- `scene.py` board (lacquer lines texture, darker sides), bowls with heaped stones, stones on points with a little jitter.
- `sprites.py OUT` the app board: `wood.png` (top-down, no denoise so the grain stays sharp), `b0-3`, `w0-5`, `shadow` → convert to `.webp` in `kami/public/kiai/board/`.
- `clouds.py OUT W H S` the sky plate: a cumulus sea 300 m below (volumes, 6 bounces so they stay white), sun 21° up. 2560×960 at 24 samples took about 9 min on 4 cores.
- `kaya_tex.py OUT_DIR [SEED]` numpy textures for the live 3D board (`kami/src/three/kaya.ts`): kaya top (masame grain matched to the reference board), front, side, grain bump, bowl mulberry (dark, like the reference's), white-stone clamshell. The broad grain bands are kept gentle so a straight-down view doesn't read as stripes. Outputs go to `kami/public/kiai/3d/` as webp.
- `hero.py OUT W H S plate` the table and sky only, for the live hero: the site draws board, stones and bowls in 3D on top with the same camera (written as JSON next to OUT; copied into `kami/src/three/plate.ts`). Composed like the wide view into `kami/public/kiai/3d/plate.webp`; phones show a crop of the same plate (`PHONE_X0`/`PHONE_W` in `plate.ts`, x 1120-2320) so the whole board and both bowls fit.
- `hero.py OUT W H S wide|macro` the board on a stone table on a transparent background; `compose_hero.py` lays it over the defocused plate with matched horizon → `hero.webp`, `hero-tall.webp`. `macro` is the close-up for the on-device section (rendered without the denoiser so the grain survives; `compose_macro.py` puts it over a defocused patch of sky).
## Brand: 気合, the seal, the font (2026-10-19)
- `tools/brand/kiai_brush.py YUJI.woff2 ALEGREYA700.woff2 ALEGREYA500.woff2` writes `kami/src/ui/brand/glyphs.ts`: the outlines of 気 and 合 from Yuji Syuku (SIL OFL, a kaisho brush font, via `npm pack @fontsource/yuji-syuku`), one hand-traced centre line per stroke in stroke order, and "Kiai" plus the loader's one line as outlines. 気's ragged dry-brush hook was replaced by a clean flick (critics read the flecks as a glitch).
- `brand/brush.ts` draws 気合: a mask of fat round-capped lines uncovers the outline stroke by stroke (about 2.3 s), with dry-brush streaks cut into the ends of sweeping strokes, very fine ragged edges and a faint bleed. Gotcha: a `<mask>` with a big `maskUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` region made Chrome reveal strokes early in the wrong place; the default mask region works.
- `brand/seal.ts`: vermilion seal with 気 in paper colour. 33 px and up use the brush 気 (worn edges from 48 px); 32 px and below use a carved, even-stroke 気 that still reads at 16 px. `kami/public/favicon.svg` is the 32 px seal.
- `brand/loader.ts`: the first screen, inlined into index.html at build time by `vite.config.ts` (`<!--kiai-loader-->`), on washi from `tools/brand/washi.py` (`kami/public/kiai/washi.webp`). First visit: writes 気合, presses the seal, shows "Kiai" and "Ask what to play in any Go position", then waits for `markReady()` (`kami/src/lib/ready.ts`, called by App and by the hero once its 3D board exists). Later visits: shows only if loading takes over 450 ms. Click or key skips; after 9 s it goes regardless.
- Font: Alegreya Sans (400/500/700/800, `@fontsource/alegreya-sans`), a humanist sans with calligraphic roots, with `font-size-adjust: 0.5` so its small x-height reads like a 16 px sans. Dop's own site keeps Archivo.
- Headless Chromium here cannot screenshot pages with the live WebGL board (the compositor stalls on SwiftShader); brand shots were taken with `--disable-webgl` (landing shows its baked fallback).
- Critics (5 Haiku a round, 0-10): round 1 5.7, round 2 7.9 (see the PR for round 3).

## Honest state
- 2026-10-17 critique (5 Haiku critics): bird 3/10, board 4/10, stones 3/10, logo 3/10.
- 2026-10-18 redesign: 10 Haiku critics a round (meaning, board vs reference, glow, premium, motion, logo, app, mobile, copy, slide detector) on stills from headless Chromium, 8 rounds; averages 4.5, 4.5, 4.2, 4.7, 4.6, 4.7, 5.0, 4.8 (round 8: meaning 6, phone 6, glow/premium/motion/copy 5, board/logo/app/slides 4). No critic reached 9. Their critiques also flip between rounds (round 7 asked for the hero to open on the answer and for holds to rest; round 8's motion critic asked for the opposite), so read single scores as noise of about ±1.
- Live board materials now: wood tint 0xf8d0a8 over the kaya map (deeper amber, matched to the reference's hue), lacquered bowls (clearcoat 0.85), stones with an offset contact shadow. What critics still ask for: a darker rim/vignette on the wood, a visible bevel in the app's straight-down view, glass (fresnel) columns and tiles, and an app board that matches the landing's life.
