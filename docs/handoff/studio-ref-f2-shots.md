Source: hub library f2 (1920x1080, 75.8 s, 30 measured shots). Measured from the 48 hub stills

unknown exact time (given as a window). Positions are shares of the frame (x from left, y from
top); "her right" = viewer's left. Head centre = centre of hair+face box; head height = hair top
to chin. Caption = black sans (Proxima/Montserrat-like, ~5.5% frame height, bold-ish), centred,
top at y≈0.04, no box; white outline only on dark backgrounds.

## Motion habits
- Almost everything is a held still. 24/30 shots show no change between stills; character never walks, camera never pans or pushes.
- Life comes from the edit: median shot ~2.4 s, 0.3 s micro-shots (2, 9, 13), 12/29 cuts on a sound hit.
- Change inside a shot = add or swap one thing on a caption beat: "2" written on banner, "!"/"Message" bubbles, "Aww!" bubble,
  sweat drip, shrimp removed. Same drawing re-used with one added element (16->17, 6->7, 1->2).
- Real movement only 4 times: mouth flap (1), whoosh smear + colour flash (9-10), shake (21), slow sway (26, 29).
- Drawings are reused: teary (12, 22, 23), cheer (18, 27, 28).
- Captions: one line per beat, replaced (not typed on), always present except reaction holds.

## Drawing style
- Line: dark maroon (#4a1e24-ish, never black), tablet-pen line ~5–7 px at 1080p, uneven pressure, tapered ends, gaps allowed.
- Fills flat, no outline on fills; no shading except shot 20. Skin pale pink #f4dcd8 (peach #ecc9bf in warm shots).
  Top: sleeveless black-brown (#4d2a2a) mock turtleneck, bare shoulders/arms.
- Hair: shoulder-length layered bob; vertical gradient dark brown-maroon (#5e2f33) at crown -> crimson/magenta (#9a3050) at tips
  (bottom third). Fringe = 5 pointed locks: outer-left to jaw, long lock over her right eye ending on the cheek, a narrow centre
  lock between the eyes to the nose bridge, a short lock over her left eye, outer-right to jaw. Two long thin flyaway strands
  stick out sideways at the top (left and right). Side ends: 3–4 sharp tips each at jaw/neck. A few interior strand lines.
- Face (front, shot 12): face width ≈ 0.63 of hair width; eye width ≈ 0.23 of face width each, eyes set at ~55% down the face,
  gap ≈ one eye width; small dot/circle nose; small pointed-round V chin. Eyes: thick upper lash line with outer flick, red irises,
  1–2 white highlights. Blush = short parallel diagonal/vertical hatch strokes, pink.
- Neck ≈ 0.4 of face width (≈0.25 of hair width); shoulders ≈ 1.9x face width (≈1.17x hair width), narrow, sloping.
- Bg flat #e4f5ff pale blue; dark solid bgs only for flash/mood shots (maroon, mauve, slate purple).
- Handwritten labels/SFX in the same maroon thin pen, lower/upper-case, wobbly, with simple arrows.
