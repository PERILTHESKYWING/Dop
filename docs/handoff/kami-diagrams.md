# Kami diagrams (built positions) and the rubric
Summary: how Kami sets up a position to explain an idea, the checks it must pass, the settings, and how XIAO's critique becomes the scorer's rubric. Added 2026-10-16 (thread "Smarter Kami AI").

## The rule (XIAO, 2026-10-16)
Kami may build positions to explain a concept, but never blindly: each one passes code checks, then KataGo reads it last, and only what that read shows may be said about it. One side must not be losing by more than the "lowest win rate" setting (default 30%).

## Files
- `src/lib/coach/explain/illustrate.ts`: `DiagramSpec`, `staticChecks` (board, legal, frame, legal), `verifyDiagram` (first read, komi, even, claim, stable, optional worse read; KataGo's final read last; a framed diagram gets one komi retry), `isolate` (cut the real fight out of the game, same coordinates; null for center fights and boards under 13x13), `frameBalance`.
- `diagrams.ts`: `illustrateDefaults(model, thinking)` (off for Hane, thinking off/low), `buildDiagrams`: since 2026-10-16 round 2 only a library example for a concept the answer actually teaches (`Teaching.topics`: double sente, reverse sente, sacrifice, tenuki, ko, reduction), with at least 10 real stones and no frame (`textbookOk`), never for overviews or problems. The game is no longer cut out and framed (`isolate`/`diagramFor` stay for the bench): XIAO, item 19, "never invent stones or walls", and item 6, "at least 10 stones". The section uses the example's own note when it has no numbers, else KataGo's fresh read.
- `writerDiagram.ts`: the writer's `DIAGRAM ... END` block. Since PROMPT_VERSION 4 the writer is no longer offered it (no invented positions); the parser stays so older answers still render, and the factory no longer makes own-diagram tasks.
- Output: `Explanation.illustrations[]` (`id` d1.., `base`, `line`, `highlight`, `view`, `checks`, `read`), shown as `[[dN]]` chips; hovering plays the diagram on the board (kami/src/ui/Messages.tsx).
- Settings › Positions (`kami/src/lib/settings.ts`): `positions.minWinrate` 30%, `positions.enabled`, `positions.writerDiagrams`, `positions.visits` 200, `positions.maxKomi` 30, `positions.maxSeconds` 12. The thinking tier still decides whether diagrams run.

## Checks, in order
board (size, points on the board) → legal (replay, no suicide, no captures left hanging) → frame (tsumego frame from `src/lib/problems/frame.ts` around the corner/side, when the spec asks for one) → legal again → first KataGo read → komi (evens the game; the local value, komi minus `frameBalance`, must be within maxKomi) → even (each side ≥ minWinrate) → claim (best move is KataGo's best or within 0.5 pts; "worse" is 0.5+ pts worse) → stable (claim holds at the final, deeper read).

## Measured (2026-10-16, Node WASM b10, 1 thread)
- Bench, Tesuji/medium, 11 positions: 3 got a diagram, most others failed "claim" (KataGo prefers another move in the cut-out). Kept strict on purpose.
- Critique batch, Tesuji/high, 28 items: 3 got a diagram (items 6, 12, 19); answers took 5-31 s. Run each item in its own process (`--only N`): one long run once hit "board size mismatch" mid-batch and it did not repeat item by item (cause not found).
- The writer's own diagram is unit-tested with a scripted engine only; not yet tried with real Gemini.

## Rubric
- `eval/coach/rubric.json` v1 (2026-10-16): 34 rules from XIAO's critique of the 28-answer batch (global rules §1-9 and notes on items 1-28); each rule's `from` names its source. Fields: `text` (the judge reads it, at most ~35 words), `weight` (3 critical or repeated, 2 important, 1 minor), `topic` (tone, teaching, accuracy, structure, diagrams), `applies` (position, concept, all), `when`, `check`. 22 rules are code-checked by `src/lib/coach/rubric.ts`; the other 12 are judge-only. Every rule's text goes to the judge (`scripts/coachlm/judge.py`, fourth number, 50%).
- Check kinds: require, forbid, words, terms, refsDiagram; leadsWithAnswer (first two sentences name the asked or best move, link a line, or give a verdict in words: Yes/No, good, slow, wide open, ahead); leadsWith (first sentence, bold and refs stripped, matches a pattern); refsLine (links one of the answer's lines or diagrams; null when it has none); maxNumbers (a copy of text.ts `numbersIn`: coordinates, refs, 3-3, 19x19, 3rd line, ranks, "4-stone" and list markers do not count); maxMatches (g added; emoji are `\p{Extended_Pictographic}` with flag u); noRepeats (the same sentence after case, punctuation and refs, a 5+ word sentence 90% inside another, or the same "X is N points worse" / "Black leads by N" twice).
- `when` (all given fields must hold, else the rule is skipped): from `Explanation.situation` tsumego, open, handicap, styleChoice, transposes (booleans) and phase, gap, tone (lists); from `question` kind, intent (lists) and `ask` (a case-insensitive pattern on the student's words). Answers made before `situation` existed skip the situation conditions; concept answers skip every `when`. Number patterns use `(?<![\w.-])` so "S14 point", "3-3 point" and "vital point" never count as points.
- `rubricText(rubric, topic?)`: the numbered list for the judge; with a topic only those rules (the tone reviewer reads `tone`).
- `verify.ts` adds `rubric` (0 when truth < 1) and `broken`; `pool.py` blends `answer_quality = 0.5·base + 0.5·rubric`. Tests: `tests/coach-rubric.test.ts`.
- Measured 2026-10-16 on the 23 old position answers XIAO critiqued (summary + sections): v0 0.938, v1 0.508 as stored, 0.468 with a hand-written situation per item. Most broken: no-repeats 23, lead-in-go-words 22, few-numbers 20, winrate-sparing 16, no-engine-report 15, relaxed-few-numbers 8, split-hairs 6. XIAO's 14 example replies score 0.93 as written (no chips) and 1.0 with one `[[v1]]` chip. By XIAO's rating the old answers score 0.46 (rated 1, n=15), 0.44 (2, n=4), 0.52 (3, n=4): weak, since his 3s still carried complaints.
- Critique items that are not rubric rules (UI or diagram builder): hover coordinates (item 3), hover definitions of Go terms (8, 11), a cropped board for tsumego (16), diagrams with 10+ stones (6), a clearer teaching line within ~2-3% of KataGo's (7), a real life-and-death solver (16).
- XIAO's critique: `scripts/kami-critique.ts --out f.json` makes the 28-item batch; the critique page stores feedback in the artifact's db, collection `feedback`, docs `item-<id>` and `item-general` ({rating, tags, text}). Read them with the ArtifactData tool; a new critique becomes rubric v2 the same way (keep `from: "XIAO, item N"`).

- Round 2 (2026-10-16): the 19 non-problem positions answered by the new teacher text score 0.995 on the code checks (old answers 0.454, same situations). Scored with `scoreRubric` (src/lib/coach/rubric.ts) on `scripts/kami-critique.ts --cache` output, next to the old batch.

## Data limits
- No free post-2022 corpus of humans explaining Go with positions exists that we found, so XIAO's rubric is the human signal. Training positions are 2022+ games only (OGS archive release, cwi title games with DT ≥ 2022, the repo's 2026 engine games); the 1940-2017 pro walks only with `--sources pro`.
